01. About Me

I build and operate the infrastructure that keeps businesses running. My focus is hybrid cloud architecture, automation, and internal tooling, creating systems that are reliable by design and easier to manage over time.

At Rimrock Capital I serve as Lead Operations Engineer, owning a hybrid AWS and Hyper-V environment end-to-end. That spans cloud infrastructure, patching and compliance, endpoint security, backup operations, and alerting pipelines built on CloudWatch, SNS, and Lambda. I write tooling in Python and PowerShell to eliminate manual overhead and use AI-assisted development to ship faster.

My career started in product support at Linksys, where I learned to diagnose problems quickly and communicate clearly across technical and non-technical teams. I brought that mindset into enterprise infrastructure and have spent the last few years applying it at increasing scale and complexity.

When I'm not working I'm usually in my homelab, running Tailscale and Pi-hole on a Raspberry Pi, experimenting with Proxmox, and hosting a few VMs including a domain controller and backup services. It keeps me sharp and gives me a safe place to try things I wouldn't touch in production.
